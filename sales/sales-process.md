# Sales Process

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## Overview

ASLF Formulas operates a **fully automated sales process**. No human sales representatives are involved. AI agents manage the entire funnel from awareness to purchase to retention, using a combination of Shopify automations, email/SMS flows, and AI-powered personalization tools.

---

## Sales Funnel Stages

### Stage 1: Acquisition
- **Traffic sources:** Paid ads (Meta, Google) managed by AI bidding algorithms; organic SEO; social content
- **Landing pages:** Shopify storefront, optimized via A/B testing tools (e.g., Google Optimize, Intelligems)
- **Lead capture:** Email/SMS opt-in via pop-ups and checkout flows (Klaviyo)

### Stage 2: Conversion
- **AI product recommendations:** Shopify's built-in recommendation engine + third-party AI upsell apps
- **Abandoned cart recovery:** Automated 3-email sequence triggered within 1h, 24h, and 72h of abandonment
- **On-site chat:** AI chatbot handles pre-purchase questions (e.g., product ingredients, shipping, discounts)
- **Discount logic:** AI-managed discount rules (first-time buyer, loyalty tier, seasonal)

### Stage 3: Post-Purchase
- **Order confirmation + tracking:** Automated email/SMS via Shopify + carrier integration
- **Upsell/Cross-sell:** Post-purchase offer page and follow-up email sequence
- **Review request:** Automated review request email sent 7 days after delivery (Okendo / Klaviyo)

### Stage 4: Retention
- **Loyalty & repeat purchase flows:** Segmented Klaviyo email flows based on purchase history
- **Win-back campaigns:** Automated re-engagement for customers inactive 60+ days
- **Subscription offers:** Where applicable, AI prompts one-time buyers to subscribe for savings

---

## AI Agent Responsibilities

| Task | AI Agent / Tool | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Abandoned cart email | Klaviyo automation | Cart abandoned >1h |
| Post-purchase upsell | Shopify app (e.g., ReConvert) | Order placed |
| Review request | Okendo / Klaviyo | 7 days post-delivery |
| Win-back email | Klaviyo flow | 60 days no purchase |
| Ad budget optimization | Meta Advantage+, Google Smart Bidding | Continuous |
| Chat / pre-sale questions | AI chatbot (e.g., Tidio AI, Shopify Sidekick) | Customer message |

---

## Escalation to Owner

AI agents handle 100% of routine sales operations autonomously. The owner is notified (via automated alert) in the following cases:

- Conversion rate drops >20% week-over-week
- A/B test reaches statistical significance and needs strategic direction
- A new sales channel or major campaign requires approval
- Revenue drops below defined threshold (see [okrs-and-kpis.md](../strategy/okrs-and-kpis.md))
