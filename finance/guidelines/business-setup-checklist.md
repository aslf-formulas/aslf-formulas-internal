### Trademark vs. Copyright — Important Distinction

Your **trademark** protects your **brand name and logo** from others using it commercially. It does NOT cover your creative works.

**Copyright** protects your **original creative content** — YouTube videos, website copy, product photos, written materials, formulas documentation. In the US, copyright is automatically applied the moment you create something, but **registering with the US Copyright Office** ($65–$85 per filing) strengthens your legal position if you ever need to enforce it (e.g., someone copies your YouTube videos or website content).

**Recommended:** Register copyrights for your key YouTube videos and website content. This is especially valuable given your growing YouTube channel.
