## 📊 Monthly P&L Report

The monthly Profit & Loss report should include:

- **Revenue:** Total income by source/product line
- **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS):** Direct costs of production
- **Gross Profit:** Revenue minus COGS
- **Operating Expenses:** All indirect business expenses
- **Net Profit / Loss:** Gross profit minus operating expenses
- **Comparison:** Current month vs. prior month and vs. budget
