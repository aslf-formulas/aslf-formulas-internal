# Internal Workflows

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## 📋 Overview

This document maps out key recurring workflows at ASLF Formulas to ensure consistency and avoid dropped tasks.

---

## 🔁 Weekly Workflows

### Monday: Weekly Kick-Off
1. Team syncs on priorities for the week
2. Finance: Review outstanding invoices
3. Operations: Check inventory levels
4. Marketing: Review previous week's content performance
5. Customer Support: Clear any outstanding tickets from the weekend

### Friday: Weekly Wrap-Up
1. Log completed tasks in project management tool
2. Update any in-progress project statuses
3. Identify blockers for the following week
4. Send weekly summary to relevant stakeholders (if applicable)

---

## 🔁 Monthly Workflows

### 1st–5th of Each Month
- [ ] Finance: Prepare and share monthly P&L report
- [ ] Finance: Review all outstanding invoices and follow up on overdue payments
- [ ] Operations: Review inventory and place restock orders if needed
- [ ] Marketing: Review last month's marketing KPIs
- [ ] Customer Support: Review support ticket volume and themes

### Mid-Month
- [ ] Marketing: Prepare content for the following month
- [ ] Finance: Process contractor payments (if bi-monthly)
- [ ] Product: Product feedback review meeting

### End of Month
- [ ] Close out any open action items
- [ ] Prep for next month's priorities

---

## 🔁 Quarterly Workflows

- Budget vs. actuals review
- Product roadmap review
- Marketing strategy review
- Customer satisfaction review
- Team performance check-ins

---

```
## [Workflow Name]

...

```
