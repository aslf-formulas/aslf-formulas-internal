# New Team Member Onboarding

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## 👋 Welcome to ASLF Formulas!

This document guides new team members through their first days and weeks at ASLF Formulas, ensuring they have everything they need to hit the ground running.

---

## ✅ Before Day 1 (Manager's Responsibility)

- [ ] Send welcome email with start date, time, and any first-day logistics
- [ ] Set up email account and tool access
- [ ] Add to relevant Slack channels / communication tools
- [ ] Add to project management tool
- [ ] Share this onboarding document
- [ ] Assign an onboarding buddy (if applicable)

---

## 📅 Week 1: Orientation

### Day 1

- [ ] Welcome meeting with manager
- [ ] Introduction to the team
- [ ] Set up all accounts and tools (see [tools-and-systems.md](./tools-and-systems.md))
- [ ] Read the [Company Overview](../company-guidelines/company-overview.md)
- [ ] Read the [Mission, Vision & Values](../company-guidelines/mission-vision-values.md)
- [ ] Read the [Code of Conduct](../company-guidelines/code-of-conduct.md)

### Days 2–5

- [ ] Deep dive into your role responsibilities and current projects
- [ ] Review relevant SOPs for your department
- [ ] Shadow team members for key processes
- [ ] Meet with key stakeholders / other departments
- [ ] Set 30/60/90-day goals with your manager

---

## 📅 Week 2–4: Getting Up to Speed

- [ ] Take ownership of first tasks or projects
- [ ] Complete any required training or certifications
- [ ] Attend all regular team meetings
- [ ] Review department documentation thoroughly
- [ ] Check in with manager for feedback and questions
