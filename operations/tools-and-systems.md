## 🔐 Security & Access Policy

- All team members must use the **company password manager** — never store passwords in browsers or personal tools
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) must be enabled on all accounts
- Access is role-based — only request access to tools you need for your role
- When a team member leaves, their access must be **revoked immediately**
