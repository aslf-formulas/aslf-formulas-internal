# Intellectual Property Ownership

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## Overview

All intellectual property created by, for, or on behalf of ASLF Formulas is owned by **ASLF LLC**. This includes work created by human contractors, AI agents, and automated systems.

---

## What We Own

| Asset Type | Examples | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Product formulas | Electrolyte blend recipes, ingredient ratios | ASLF LLC |
| Brand assets | Logo, name "ASLF Formulas", brand colors, packaging design | ASLF LLC |
| Written content | Website copy, email content, social media posts | ASLF LLC |
| AI-generated content | Text, images, designs created using AI tools | ASLF LLC |
| Software & automations | Custom Shopify scripts, automation workflows, AI agent configurations | ASLF LLC |
| Customer data | Order history, contact data, behavioral data | ASLF LLC (subject to privacy policy) |
| Trade secrets | Supplier relationships, pricing structures, internal processes | ASLF LLC |

---

## AI-Generated Content

ASLF Formulas uses AI agents to generate content including marketing copy, product descriptions, social posts, and operational documents. All AI-generated outputs created in the course of operating the business are owned by **ASLF LLC**.

**Policy:**
- AI tools used on behalf of the company must operate under ASLF LLC's accounts
- No AI-generated content may be attributed to the AI tool — all content is published as ASLF Formulas content
- AI-generated formulas or product improvements are treated as trade secrets and documented accordingly

---

## Third-Party Work

Any contractor, agency, or service provider who creates work for ASLF Formulas must assign all IP rights to ASLF LLC as part of their contractor agreement (see [contracts-and-nda.md](./contracts-and-nda.md)).

---

## Formula Protection

Product formulas are our core trade secret. They are:
- Stored in encrypted, access-controlled systems only
- Shared only under NDA
- Never disclosed in public-facing content
- Owned entirely by ASLF LLC, regardless of who or what developed them
