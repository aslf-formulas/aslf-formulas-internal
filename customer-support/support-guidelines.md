# Customer Support Guidelines

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## 🎯 Our Support Philosophy

At ASLF Formulas, we believe that **every customer interaction is an opportunity to build trust**. Our support team represents the company — and the way we handle questions, problems, and feedback directly impacts customer loyalty.

**Our standards:**
- Respond with empathy and professionalism
- Be honest — don't overpromise
- Solve the problem, not just the symptom
- Follow up to ensure satisfaction

---

## ⏱️ Response Time Targets

| Channel | Target Response Time |
|---|---|
| Email | Within 24 hours (business days) |
| Live chat | Within 5 minutes |
| Social media DMs | Within 4 hours (business hours) |
| Phone | Answer within 3 rings (if applicable) |

If you cannot fully resolve an issue within the target time, **acknowledge the customer's message** and set expectations for when they'll hear back.

---

## 🗣️ Communication Standards

### Tone
- **Warm and human** — not robotic or corporate
- **Professional** — always polished, even when the customer is upset
- **Clear** — avoid jargon; explain things simply

### Language
- Use the customer's name when possible
- Always thank them for reaching out
- Apologize when appropriate — but don't admit legal fault without authorization
- End every interaction with a clear next step or resolution confirmation

### ✅ Do
- "I completely understand your frustration, and I'm here to help."
- "Let me look into this for you right away."
- "Thank you for bringing this to our attention."

### ❌ Don't
- "That's not our fault."
- "You should have read the policy."
- Leave a message unread or unacknowledged.
