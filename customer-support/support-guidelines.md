# Customer Support Guidelines

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## Overview

ASLF Formulas customer support is handled **entirely by AI agents**, operating 24/7. The AI agent resolves the vast majority of issues autonomously using approved scripts and logic. The owner is only involved for escalated edge cases that require human judgment or legal authority.

---

## Response Time Targets

| Channel | Target Response Time | Handled By |
|---|---|---|
| Email | Within 2 hours | AI agent (automated) |
| Live chat | Instant | AI agent (automated) |
| Social media DMs | Within 1 hour | AI agent (automated) |

All channels are monitored and responded to automatically. There are no business hours — support runs 24/7.

---

## AI Agent Communication Standards

All customer-facing responses from the AI agent must reflect these standards:

### Tone
- Warm and helpful — never dismissive or robotic
- Clear and concise — no jargon
- Solution-oriented — focus on resolving the issue, not explaining policy

### Language
- Address the customer by name
- Thank them for reaching out
- Apologize when appropriate (without admitting legal fault)
- End every interaction with a clear resolution or next step

### Do
- "I completely understand — let me get this sorted for you right away."
- "Thank you for reaching out. Here's what I can do..."
- Offer a concrete resolution (replacement, refund, tracking info) in the first response when possible

### Don't
- "That's not our policy."
- Leave a message unacknowledged
- Make commitments outside the approved resolution scripts

## Data & Privacy

- The AI agent never shares customer personal information with unauthorized parties
- Account changes require identity verification via order number + email match (automated check)
- Data deletion requests are logged automatically and forwarded to the owner for processing
- All customer data is handled per the privacy policy at aslf-formulas.com/policies/privacy-policy
