**[STORY 1 — 0:08–0:40]**
**FDA approves the first epigenetic reprogramming human trial**

"For the first time in history, a gene therapy that literally resets aging cells is being tested inside a living human. Life Biosciences — co-founded by Harvard's David Sinclair — just got FDA clearance for their therapy ER-100. It uses three genes to tell your cells to go back to a younger state. They're starting in the eye — targeting glaucoma and optic nerve damage — but the real goal is the whole body. If this works, every organ becomes a target. This is the trial the entire field has been waiting for."

---

**[STORY 2 — 0:40–1:10]**
**Scientists just redefined what aging actually is**

"At the World Longevity Congress in Berlin this week, researchers are saying we've had it wrong. Aging isn't a defect. It's not your cells breaking down in isolation. It's your biological systems losing the ability to coordinate with each other — your immune system, your hormones, your metabolism all slowly falling out of sync. Why does this matter? Because it changes the whole treatment strategy. Instead of targeting one pathway, the future is about restoring communication between systems. That shift in thinking is going to reshape everything."

---

**[STORY 3 — 1:10–1:45]**
**A simple drug combo extended lifespan 73% in old mice**

"UC Berkeley researchers took mice that were the equivalent of 75-year-old humans — already frail — and gave them a combination of oxytocin and an anti-inflammatory compound. The male mice lived 73% longer from that point. They got stronger. Better memory. More agile. Here's what's wild: oxytocin is already FDA-approved. The other compound is already in human trials. The translation timeline is much shorter than a brand new drug. Oh — and it only worked in males, not females. Which tells us sex-specific aging biology is something the field is going to have to take a lot more seriously."

---

**[STORY 4 — 1:45–2:10]**
**GLP-1 drugs — the same class as Ozempic — are now being tested for longevity**

"You've heard of GLP-1s for weight loss. Now they're being tested in combination therapies aimed at extending healthy lifespan — targeting inflammation, metabolic decline, and cellular aging. First human trials start later this year. This matters because GLP-1 drugs already have massive clinical infrastructure and reach tens of millions of people. If this works, longevity medicine doesn't stay in niche clinics. It goes mainstream."

---

**[STORY 5 — 2:10–2:35]**
**The U.S. government just committed $144 million to extend human healthspan**

"ARPA-H — think DARPA but for medicine — announced $144 million in funding for seven research teams specifically focused on extending the number of healthy years people live. One team, Cambrian Bio, got $30 million alone to develop a cleaner version of rapamycin — the longevity benefits, without the side effects. When the federal government starts using the phrase 'healthspan industry,' something has shifted. This is no longer fringe science."

---

**[BONUS — 2:35–2:55]**
**Vitamin D3 cuts biological aging by nearly 3 years**

"Bonus story — and this one applies to you right now. A study found that consistent daily Vitamin D3 supplementation reduces biological wear equivalent to nearly three years of aging. Not just correcting a deficiency — active daily supplementation. It's one of the cheapest, most accessible things you can do. If you're not taking it, start."
