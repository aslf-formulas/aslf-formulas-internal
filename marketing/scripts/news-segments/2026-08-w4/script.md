[STORY 3 — 1:18–1:58]
**That “we’ll live to 150” headline, said honestly**

"Number 3. A headline has been going around that says humans can live to 150. That is not happening now. The average person still lives about 74 years. What the scientists actually did is a thought experiment on a computer. They modeled leftover typos in our DNA — the random damage that piles up in cells over a lifetime. They asked: if we someday fixed aging, and only those typos were left, would people just keep going forever? Their model said no. That leftover wear, especially in the brain and the heart, would still catch most people around 150. So the story is not 'we made it to 150.' And it is not a law that nobody can ever pass it. It is one team's guess at where the road might still end — much later than today, but not forever."
