NewLimit — co-founded by Coinbase's Brian Armstrong — closed the round, led by Peter Thiel's Founders Fund. The bet is cellular reprogramming: resetting old cells to act young again. [STORY 2 — 0:36–1:04]
**Flipping one gene rewound aging in the liver**

"Number 2. Scientists flipped a single gene and rewound aging in the liver — at least in mice. As we age, the way our DNA is packaged drifts, switching the wrong genes on and off. Boosting a protein called SIRT6 in old mice didn't just slow that drift — it reversed it toward a youthful pattern, lowering inflammation and improving how the liver handled energy. One gene, nudging the cell's software back toward young."

---

[STORY 3 — 1:04–1:33]
**The 'good' longevity gene that protects brain cells**

"Number 3. There's a gene variant linked to living longer — and we're learning how it guards the brain. It's called APOE2. In the lab, neurons carrying it repaired their DNA better and resisted aging, compared to neurons with APOE4 — the variant tied to higher Alzheimer's risk. The same protection showed up in aged mice. You can't pick your genes. But understanding why APOE2 protects could point to ways to mimic it."
