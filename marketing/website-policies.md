## Required Policies

### 1. Privacy Policy *(Required by law)*

**Why it's required:** Any website collecting personal data (name, email, payment info, IP address) from US customers — and especially California residents — must have a Privacy Policy under:
- **CalOPPA** (California Online Privacy Protection Act)
- **CCPA** (California Consumer Privacy Act)
- **Shopify's Terms of Service** (Shopify requires all stores to have one)

**What to include:**
- What data is collected (name, email, shipping address, payment info, cookies, IP)
- How it is used (order fulfillment, marketing, analytics)
- Whether it is shared with third parties (Shopify, payment processors, email platforms)
- Customer rights (opt-out, data deletion requests)
- Contact information for privacy inquiries
- Effective date

**Recommended approach:** Use Shopify's built-in Privacy Policy generator (Settings → Policies → Privacy Policy) as a starting point, then customize.

---

### 2. Terms of Service / Terms & Conditions *(Strongly recommended)*

**Why it's important:**
- Limits your liability
- Defines what customers can and cannot do
- Sets expectations on returns, refunds, and disputes
- Protects intellectual property

**What to include:**
- Acceptance of terms
- Product descriptions and pricing
- Order process and payment
- Shipping and delivery
- Returns and refund policy
- Prohibited uses
- Intellectual property rights
- Limitation of liability
- Governing law (Texas)
- Contact information

**Recommended approach:** Use Shopify's Terms of Service generator as a starting point.

---

### 3. Refund / Return Policy *(Required by Shopify)*

- Clearly state your return window (e.g., 30 days)
- Conditions for returns (unopened, original packaging)
- Process for requesting a refund
- Who pays return shipping

---

### 4. Shipping Policy

- Processing times
- Carriers used
- Estimated delivery windows
- International shipping (if applicable)
- Lost/damaged package process

---

### 5. CAN-SPAM Compliance *(For email marketing)*

If sending marketing emails:
- Always include a physical mailing address
- Always include an unsubscribe link
- Honor unsubscribe requests within 10 business days
- Never use deceptive subject lines
