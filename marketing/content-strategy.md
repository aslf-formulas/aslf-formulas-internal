## ✍️ Content Creation Process

1. **Ideation** — Brainstorm ideas based on content pillars and audience questions
2. **Planning** — Add to the content calendar at least 2 weeks ahead
3. **Creation** — Draft, design, or film the content
4. **Review** — At least one team member reviews before publishing
5. **Publishing** — Schedule or post at optimal times for each platform
6. **Reporting** — Track performance 7 and 30 days post-publish
