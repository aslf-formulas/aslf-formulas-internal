# Accessibility Statement — ASLF Formulas

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

At ASLF Formulas, we believe everyone deserves access to quality health and wellness products and information — regardless of ability. That commitment extends to our online experience.

---

## Our Commitment

We are dedicated to making our website ([aslf-formulas.com](https://aslf-formulas.com/)) accessible to all visitors, including individuals with disabilities.

Our site is built on **Shopify's Dawn theme**, which includes accessibility-friendly features such as:

- Keyboard navigation support
- Readable color contrast ratios
- Screen-reader compatibility
- Responsive design for all device sizes

---

## Standard We Follow

We continually review and improve our site following the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA** — the recognized global standard for digital accessibility.

---

## Known Limitations

We are actively working to improve accessibility across all pages. Some third-party content or embedded tools may not yet fully meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards. We are committed to addressing these as they are identified.
