# Shipping Policy — ASLF Formulas

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

Thank you for shopping with ASLF Formulas. Here is everything you need to know about how we process and ship your orders.

---

## Order Processing Time

All orders are processed within **1 business day** (Monday–Friday, excluding federal holidays). You will receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number once your order has shipped.

> Orders placed on weekends or holidays will begin processing the next business day.

---

## Shipping Estimates

### Domestic (United States)

| Service | Estimated Delivery | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Shipping | 2–6 business days | $4.90–$9.90 |
| Express Shipping | Calculated at checkout | Varies by location |

### International

| Service | Estimated Delivery | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| International Standard | 7–16 business days | $19.90 |

> **Note:** Delivery estimates begin from the date of shipment, not the date of order. Processing time is additional.

---

## Shipping Carriers

We ship via **USPS, UPS, and FedEx** depending on your location and the selected service. Carrier selection is at our discretion to ensure the best delivery experience.

---

## Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive an email with your **tracking number**. You can track your package directly on the carrier's website or through the link in your shipping confirmation email.

---

## Large Order Surcharge

Additional shipping fees may apply for orders over **5 lbs**. If applicable, this will be calculated and shown at checkout before you complete your purchase.

---

## Shipping Delays

While we do everything possible to meet estimated delivery windows, delays may occasionally occur due to:

- High order volume
- Carrier delays or disruptions
- Severe weather conditions
- Inaccurate or incomplete shipping addresses
- Customs processing (international orders)

We appreciate your patience. If your order is significantly delayed, please contact us and we will do our best to help.

---

## International Shipping — Customs, Duties & Taxes

All international orders are shipped from **Austin, Texas, USA**. Your order may be subject to import duties, taxes, and brokerage fees imposed by your country's customs authority.

- These charges are the **responsibility of the recipient** and are not included in the purchase price or shipping fees
- We are unable to predict or cover these charges
- We recommend checking with your local customs office before placing your order
- Customs fees are **non-refundable**

---

## Lost or Stolen Packages

- If your tracking shows **delivered** but you have not received your package, please first check with neighbors and your local carrier
- Contact us at **contact@aslf-formulas.com** within **7 days of the marked delivery date**
- We will work with the carrier to investigate and, where applicable, reship or refund your order

> We are not responsible for packages stolen after confirmed delivery. We strongly recommend shipping to a secure location.

---

## Wrong Address

Please double-check your shipping address before completing your order. If you provided an incorrect address:

- Contact us **immediately** at contact@aslf-formulas.com — we may be able to correct it before the order ships
- If the order has already shipped and is returned to us due to an incorrect address, re-shipment fees will apply
- We are not responsible for orders delivered to an incorrectly entered address
