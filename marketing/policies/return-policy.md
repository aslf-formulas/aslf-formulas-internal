# Return & Refund Policy — ASLF Formulas

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not satisfied, we’re here to help.

---

## Return Window

We accept returns within **30 days** of the delivery date.

---

## Eligibility

To be eligible for a return, your item must be:

- **Unused and in original condition** — unopened, with all original packaging and seals intact
- **OR defective / damaged** — if your product arrived damaged or defective, it qualifies regardless of condition

> **Note:** Opened or partially used products are not eligible for return unless defective.

---

## Non-Returnable Items

- Opened supplement products (unless defective)
- Items not in original packaging
- Items returned after the 30-day window
- Items purchased as final sale

---

## How to Initiate a Return

1. **Email us** at **contact@aslf-formulas.com** with subject line: *"Return Request — Order #[your order number]"*
2. Include: your order number, the item(s) you wish to return, and the reason
3. You’ll receive return instructions within **24 hours**
4. **Do not ship items back without authorization** — unauthorized returns may not be processed

---

## Return Shipping

- **Customer is responsible** for return shipping costs for standard returns (change of mind, ordered wrong item)
- **We cover return shipping** for defective or incorrectly shipped items — a prepaid label will be provided
- Use a trackable shipping method. We are not responsible for returns lost in transit without tracking

## Refunds

- Once your return is received and inspected, you’ll be notified by email
- If approved, your refund will be processed within **5–7 business days**
- Refunds are issued to the **original payment method**
- Shipping costs are non-refundable (except for defective or incorrectly shipped items)

## Damaged or Wrong Items

If you received a damaged, defective, or incorrect item:

1. Email us at **contact@aslf-formulas.com** within **7 days of delivery**
2. Include photos of the damage or incorrect item
3. We’ll send a replacement or issue a full refund — your choice, at no cost to you

We do not currently offer direct exchanges. To get a different item, return the original and place a new order.
