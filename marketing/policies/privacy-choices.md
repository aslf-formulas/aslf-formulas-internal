## Your Privacy Rights

At ASLF Formulas, we do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for targeted advertising or marketing purposes.

We collect personal information from your interactions with us and our website, including through cookies and similar technologies, solely to fulfill your orders, improve your shopping experience, and provide customer support. For full details, please review our [Privacy Policy](https://aslf-formulas.com/policies/privacy-policy).

## Your Right to Opt Out

Even though we do not sell or share your data for advertising, you still have the following privacy controls available:

1. **Email us directly:** Send a request to contact@aslf-formulas.com with the subject line "Privacy Request." We will respond within 15 business days.

2. **Global Privacy Control (GPC):** If you visit our website with the GPC signal enabled in your browser, we will honor it as a request to limit any data processing that could be considered sharing. Learn more at [globalprivacycontrol.org](https://globalprivacycontrol.org).

3. **Cookie settings:** You can manage or disable non-essential cookies through your browser settings at any time.

## California Residents — Additional Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:
- Know what personal information we have collected about you
- Request deletion of your personal information
- Request correction of inaccurate personal information
- Not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **contact@aslf-formulas.com** with the subject line "CCPA Privacy Request."
