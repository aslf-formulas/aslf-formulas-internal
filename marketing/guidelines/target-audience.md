# Target Audience

> *ASLF Formulas is the work of a longevity analyst who cares about the field
> so much that he built a clean product line he'd actually trust. The editorial
> mission leads; the products exist because of it, not the other way around.*
>
> Full positioning: [../../company-guidelines/positioning.md](../../company-guidelines/positioning.md).
>
> **Last updated: June 2026**

---

## Audience First, Customer Second

We build an **audience** before we build a customer base. Trust earned as the
longevity analyst is what makes the product line credible. Most of our audience
will never buy — that's fine and expected; they're the people who keep us
honest and who share us to keep others current.

---

## The Follower We're Building For

**"The longevity-curious adult who doesn't want to fall behind."**

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Age Range** | ~30–55 |
| **Mindset** | Science-curious, wellness-oriented, educated. Reads/listens to credible sources (the Veritasium / Huberman / The Drive type). |
| **Where they are** | YouTube, TikTok, X, Instagram |

### The job they hire us for
- *"Keep me current on longevity without my having to read journals and a dozen
  newsletters myself."*
- *"Tell me which of this week's fifty headlines actually matter — and how they
  connect."*
- *"Be the one who won't oversell me."*

### Pain points we solve
- Information overload + disconnection — fragments with no map.
- Hype fatigue — overclaiming and supplement-shilling everywhere.
- Not knowing whom to trust.

### What earns their trust
- Connective analysis, cited claims, qualified findings, honesty about our own
  categories, and consistency (showing up every week).

---

## Customer (downstream of the audience)

The subset who, having come to trust the analysis, choose the products because
they want a clean, transparent option from someone who clearly cares. We never
invert this order in messaging.

---

## Who We Do NOT Serve

- People seeking miracle cures or guaranteed outcomes — we will disappoint them
  on purpose (positioning.md §7).
- People wanting a guru's fixed protocol or stack to follow blindly.
