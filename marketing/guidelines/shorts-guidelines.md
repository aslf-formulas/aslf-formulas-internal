# Short-Form Video Clips — Process & Guidelines

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## Overview

This document defines the process for repurposing long-form YouTube video transcripts into high-performing, standalone vertical video clips optimized for X (Twitter), YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels.

The AI agent (Claude) processes SRT transcript files and extracts clip recommendations following the guidelines below. The owner reviews the output and selects which clips to produce.

---

## How to Use

1. Export the SRT file from your video editing tool or YouTube Studio
2. Paste the SRT content and ask Claude: *"Extract shorts from this SRT following the shorts guidelines"*
3. Claude returns a numbered list of clips with timestamps, transcript, caption, and hashtags
4. Owner selects clips and hands off to editing (manual or AI-assisted)
5. Log completed clips in the [Completed Clips Log](#completed-clips-log) below

---

## Core Rules

- Every clip must be **completely self-contained** and deliver clear, immediate value (actionable tip, surprising fact, strong insight, motivational moment, powerful quote, or direct call-to-action)
- **No overlapping timestamps** between clips
- **No filler, rambling, or low-value moments**
- Clips can be as short as needed as long as they communicate a solid idea
- All content must be 100% accurate, positive, educational, and fully compliant with all platform community guidelines
- Never promote misinformation, harm, or controversy for engagement
- Prioritize clips that naturally encourage saves, shares, comments, or follows

---

## AI Agent Workflow

1. Read the entire transcript carefully
2. Identify natural topic shifts, punchy statements, surprising statistics, actionable advice, emotional peaks, quotes, or strong calls-to-action
3. Extract distinct, non-overlapping segments that enclose topics end to end
4. Segments do not need to start right after one another — skip less valuable parts

---

## Output Format

For each clip, provide in this exact order:

```
Short #[N]
- Timestamps: Start: [H:MM:SS] → End: [H:MM:SS]
- Transcript: [exact verbatim text of the selected segment only]
- Caption: [concise, natural, human-sounding, value-first caption — max 280 characters, ideally 100–180. Conversational but authoritative tone. End with a subtle or direct CTA when organic. NO emojis.]
- Hashtags: [exactly 10–15 relevant, high-search-volume hashtags. Short common words or tight phrases strongly related to clip content. Each starts with #, no spaces inside, separated by single spaces.]
```

---

## Caption Guidelines

- Max 280 characters, ideally 100–180
- Conversational but authoritative tone
- Value-first — lead with the insight, not a question
- End with a subtle or direct call-to-action when it feels organic
- **No emojis**
- Do not start with "I" or the brand name

---

## Hashtag Guidelines

- Exactly 10–15 hashtags per clip
- Use short, common single words or tight phrases that appear in or are strongly related to the clip content
- High search volume preferred
- Each hashtag starts with `#`, no spaces inside
- Separated by single spaces

---

## Example Output

Short #1
