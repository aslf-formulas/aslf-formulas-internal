## Content Pillars

All content is organized around four pillars. The **majority is analyst content**
(Educate + Connect); product content (Sell) is a clearly fenced minority.

| Pillar | Description | Handle |
|---|---|---|
| **Educate** | Explain the science clearly — mechanisms, findings, what they mean. The legibility mission. | Analyst |
| **Connect** | The differentiator: link studies + funding + policy into what they mean *together* (the map, not the list — positioning.md §4). Also community connection. | Analyst |
| **Transform** | Honest takes, myth-correction, before/after of *understanding* — radical honesty including our own categories (positioning.md §7.1). | Analyst |
| **Sell** | Product/commerce. Its own mode, clearly fenced, never disguised as analysis (positioning.md §6). | Brand |

---

## The Content Split (the ratio is the message)

- **Analyst content = the large majority**, including many topics we sell
  nothing for. When most of what we make has nothing to sell, the audience
  concludes on its own that we're here for the field.
- **Product content = a clear minority.** Target starting point: no more than
  **~1 in 5** analyst-handle pieces is product-led. Tune with data.
- Selling lives on the **brand handle**; the analyst handle keeps product fenced
  and rare.

## Format → Role Mapping

| Format | Role | Where authority/habit is built |
|---|---|---|
| **Weekly longevity news** | The recurring **habit** — the appointment franchise. | The anchor that brings people back every week |
| **Long-form (YouTube)** | Where **authority compounds** — connective theses prove depth. | Deepest trust; the hair-science anchor goes here |
| **Shorts (YT/TikTok)** | **Reach** — one mechanism or one connection, explained. | Top-of-funnel that feeds news + long-form |
| **X** | Fast takes, single data points, links. Text-first. | Speed + connective commentary |
| **Instagram (analyst)** | Carousels — excellent for connective analysis laid across slides. | Synthesis in native form |

*(Topic-to-format routing — what depth each topic earns — will be governed by
the forthcoming information-pipeline doc/agent.)*

## Content Creation Process

1. **Ingest** — monitor sources (see longevity-news-strategy.md + pipeline doc)
2. **Triage** — decide what each topic earns (X / short / carousel / long-form)
3. **Draft** — in the analyst voice (brand-identity.md); apply positioning.md
4. **Compliance review** — mandatory AGT-018 gate (brand expression, not just risk)
5. **Produce & publish** — native to each platform; consistent slot per platform
6. **Measure** — 24h + 7d checkpoints; habit-and-trust signals (positioning.md §9)
