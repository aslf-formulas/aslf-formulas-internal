# Contracts & NDAs

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## Overview

All vendor, supplier, and contractor relationships require a signed agreement before any work begins or products are ordered. ASLF Formulas uses standardized contract templates reviewed by legal counsel.

---

## Contract Types

### 1. Supplier / Manufacturer Agreement
Used for: contract manufacturers, ingredient suppliers, co-packers.

**Key clauses:**
- Product specifications and quality standards
- Minimum order quantities (MOQs) and lead times
- Third-party testing requirements (Certificate of Analysis required per batch)
- Confidentiality of formulas and product specs
- Liability and indemnification
- Termination conditions

**Template location:** *[Google Drive / Legal folder — link TBD]*

---

### 2. Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
Used for: any third party who receives access to proprietary formulas, business strategies, or confidential data.

**Covers:**
- Formulas and product development details
- Business financials and strategies
- Customer data and marketing strategies
- AI systems, automation configurations, and internal tooling

**Template location:** *[Google Drive / Legal folder — link TBD]*

**Process:**
1. Identify need for NDA (vendor, consultant, partner, or contractor)
2. Send NDA template via DocuSign (or equivalent e-signature tool)
3. Receive signed copy and store in Legal folder
4. Only share confidential information after NDA is executed

---

### 3. Service Provider / Contractor Agreement
Used for: any paid individual or agency performing work for ASLF Formulas (including AI service providers).

**Key clauses:**
- Scope of work
- Payment terms
- IP ownership (all work product belongs to ASLF LLC — see [ip-ownership.md](./ip-ownership.md))
- Confidentiality
- Termination

---

### 4. Terms of Service & Privacy Policy (Customer-Facing)
Published on aslf-formulas.com. Governs the customer relationship.

- **Terms of Service:** *[aslf-formulas.com/policies/terms-of-service]*
- **Privacy Policy:** *[aslf-formulas.com/policies/privacy-policy]*
- **Refund Policy:** *[aslf-formulas.com/policies/refund-policy]*

These policies are managed via Shopify and reviewed annually (or when legal requirements change).

---

## Contract Management Process

1. Identify contract need
2. Select or draft appropriate template
3. Review with legal counsel if non-standard or high-value
4. Send for signature via e-signature tool
5. Store signed contract in Legal folder (named: `[Vendor Name] - [Contract Type] - [Date].pdf`)
6. Set a calendar reminder for contract renewal date
