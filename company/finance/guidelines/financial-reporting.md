# Financial Reporting

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## Overview

All financial reports at ASLF Formulas are **generated automatically** by AI agents and accounting tools. The owner receives reports on a defined schedule and reviews them — no manual report creation required.

---

## Reporting Schedule

| Report | Frequency | Delivery | Generated By |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cash Flow Summary | Weekly | Every Monday | Accounting tool + AI agent |
| Profit & Loss (P&L) | Monthly | 2nd of each month | Accounting tool + AI agent |
| Accounts Receivable | Monthly | 2nd of each month | Accounting tool |
| Budget vs. Actuals | Monthly | 2nd of each month | AI agent |
| Quarterly Financial Review | Quarterly | 10th of Q+1 month | AI agent summary |
| Annual Report | Annually | *[Date]* | AI agent summary |

All reports are delivered to the owner automatically. The owner reviews and decides if any action is needed.

---

## Monthly P&L Report

The monthly P&L report (auto-generated) includes:

- **Revenue:** Total income by source / product line
- **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS):** Direct costs of production
- **Gross Profit:** Revenue minus COGS
- **Operating Expenses:** All indirect business expenses
- **Net Profit / Loss:** Gross profit minus operating expenses
- **Comparison:** Current month vs. prior month and vs. budget
