## About ASLF Formulas

ASLF Formulas is a longevity-media operation with a science-first product line.
The founder works as a **longevity analyst** — curating, connecting, and
explaining what's actually happening across longevity science and industry —
and built a small line of clean, third-party-tested longevity products out of
that same conviction. The editorial mission comes first; the products are the
proof of it.

**What makes us different:** we don't just report the news, we *connect* it —
the map, not the list (positioning.md §4). We cover longevity as an industry
(funding, trials, policy), not only as science. And we are radically honest,
including about our own product categories — "the one who won't oversell you"
in a space full of people who do.
