# OKRs & KPIs

*Last updated: March 2026*

## Overview

ASLF Formulas uses a lightweight **OKR (Objectives & Key Results)** framework to set quarterly direction, combined with **real-time KPI monitoring** by AI agents. The owner sets OKRs each quarter; AI agents track KPIs continuously and surface anomalies automatically.

## Standing KPIs (Monitored Continuously by AI Agents)

### Revenue & Finance
| KPI | Target | Alert Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly revenue | $10K | <$5K → owner alert |
| Gross margin | ≥65% | <55% → owner alert |
| Ad spend / revenue ratio | ≤25% | >35% → owner alert |

### Marketing
| KPI | Target | Alert Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| ROAS (blended) | ≥3x | <2x → review |
| Email open rate | ≥30% | <20% → review |
| Email click rate | ≥3% | <1.5% → review |
| Abandoned cart recovery rate | ≥10% | <5% → review |

### Customer
| KPI | Target | Alert Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Repeat purchase rate | ≥25% | <15% → review |
| Churn rate (90-day) | <30% | >45% → review |
| Support ticket resolution time | <24h | >48h → escalate |
| Review rating | ≥4.5 | <4.0 → review |

### Operations
| KPI | Target | Alert Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Order fulfillment time | ≤2 business days | >3 days → alert |
| Inventory days on hand | 30–90 days | <15 days → reorder trigger |
| CoA pass rate | 100% | Any failure → hold inventory |

## OKR Review Cadence

| Cadence | Activity |
|---|---|
| Weekly | AI agent generates KPI snapshot report |
| Monthly | Owner reviews KPI trends and adjusts tactics |
| Quarterly | Owner sets new OKRs, reviews previous quarter results |
| Annually | Full business review and strategic planning |
